Centralized SD-WAN Orchestration is one of [[SD-WAN]]'s greatest operational advantages. Instead of configuring routers individually, CLI manually and site-by-site change, architects can now use centralized controllers and orchestrators for deployment and operations

Benefits include:
1. Global visibility
2. Policy consistency
3. Template deployment
4. [[Zero-touch provisioning (ZTP)]]
5. Simplified operations

The benefits tend to grow the larger the organization, such as for multinational deployments or larger branch footprints
