SD-WAN does not replace QoS, instead SD-WAN improves on WAN intelligence, QoS remains important especially on:
- constrained liks
- MPLS
- branch uplinks
- real-time applications

SD-WAN often combines [[Application-aware routing|Application_Steering]], QoS policy and traffic shaping together
