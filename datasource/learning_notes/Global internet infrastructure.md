## POPs
Point of presence. Essentially a carrier network node where services are delivered and interconnected. 

Functions:
1. Customer access
2. Routing
3. Peering
4. Cloud interconnect
5. Backbone aggregation

Major carriers have POPs globally. The more strategic the POP footprint, the better the global service delivery, lower the latency and easier cloud interconnect becomes

---
## Submarine cables 
Internet is mostly underwater fibre. Submarine cables connect continents. Some notable cable systems include SEA-ME-WE, APCN, SJC, Unity, FASTER, etc. Carriers with strong cable ownership or control gain lower latency, higher resilience, better economics and path diversity. Singapore in particular is a major cable landing hub, regional interconnect hub and close to ASEAN growth markets.
