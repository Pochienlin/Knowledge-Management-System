# Why SD-WAN?
Traditional WANs had major problems:
- [[MPLS]] is expensive
- Internet is unpredictable
- cloud traffic is inefficeint
- branch deployment is slow
- operations are highly manual 

Enterprises want cheaper bandwidth, optimized cloud, centralized visibility, faster deployment and transport flexibility. SD-WAN emerged to abstract WAN transport into a centrally managed, intelligent overlay. This includes MPLS, Broadband, DIA, LTE/5G to all be usable simultaneously

>[!tip]
> Customers rarely buy "pure SD-WAN", instead they buy combinations such as SD-WAN + DIA, Broadband, SASE, Hybrid WAN, ZTP + managed-SD-WAN etc
