Enterprise manages appliances, policy, operations, troubleshooting, and gets more control and customization at the cost of operational complexity and staffing requirements.

Managed SD-WAN tends to provide abstracted operations, global support and faster rollout at the expense of direct control and potential provider dependency.

When companies already have an in-house network team that is capable enough to run SD-WAN, or if compliance or similar reasons leads organization to look for more control, it is possible that an organization may choose to opt for DIY instead of a managed service
