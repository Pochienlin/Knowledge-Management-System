Enterprise WANs must survive:
- Circuit failures
- ISP outages
- Fibre cuts
- Congestion
- Cloud path degradation

SD-WAN improves resilience through
- multi-link diversity
- rapid failover
- transport abstraction
- application steering

Enterprise design tend to have MPLS, broadband, LTE/5G backup all on [[Active-active pathing]] simultaneously for improved resilience and uptime
